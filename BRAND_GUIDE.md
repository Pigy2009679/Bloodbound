# BLOODBOUND — Visual brand guide

## Identity

**Name:** BLOODBOUND  
**Subtitle:** The Forbidden Art of Blood  
**Genre:** Dark fantasy magic  
**Mood:** Ancient, ominous, elegant, supernatural

The project should feel like a coherent magic discipline—not a random collection of red effects.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Background | Obsidian | `#100B0E` |
| Surface | Black cherry | `#1B1016` |
| Primary magic | Deep crimson | `#8F172B` |
| Bright liquid highlight | Ruby | `#D7354B` |
| Muted text | Ash rose | `#B9A6AA` |
| Main text | Bone white | `#F2E9EB` |

Use bright ruby sparingly. Most of the effect should remain deep crimson so the magic reads clearly without washing out the scene.

## Liquid motion language

- Prefer continuous ribbons and streams over isolated dots.
- Add subtle ripples and changing thickness to suggest fluid motion.
- Keep the flow direction legible: energy should appear to travel toward the caster's hand.
- The forearm aura should orbit the arm and respond smoothly to hand movement.
- Fade and clean up effects cleanly when casting ends.
- Avoid excessive glow, screen-filling fog, and dense particle clouds.

## VR readability

- Keep important effects visible from the player's normal viewpoint.
- Avoid effects that obscure the hand, weapon, or target.
- Avoid sudden full-screen flashes and distracting camera effects.
- Keep motion smooth and avoid visual noise.
- Profile transparency and overdraw on Quest hardware.

## Sound direction

Use original sound design: low magical resonance, a soft fluid movement layer, and restrained activation/release cues. Do not use unlicensed sounds from other games or media.

## Logo direction

A simple original mark: a narrow crimson sigil or droplet-like rune framed by a subtle gothic circle. It should remain readable as a small mod icon. Do not imitate Blade & Sorcery, Warpfrog, or another creator's logo.
